# Emax Electronics Enterprises
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Version:** 1.2  
**Status:** Recommended baseline for owner approval  
**Product:** Responsive website and web application  
**Primary market:** Lagos, Nigeria  
**Secondary market:** Other parts of Nigeria

---

## 1. Purpose
Define what the Emax website must do, who it serves, what Version 1 includes, and how success will be measured. This PRD is the controlling product document for the Technical Requirements, App Flow, UI/UX Brief, Backend Schema and Implementation Plan.

## 2. Product vision
Emax Electronics Enterprises will provide customers with convenient access to reliable lithium battery-building components, solar-related accessories and e-bikes, supported by product information, stock-status transparency and practical technical guidance.

## 3. Problem and opportunity
Customers may encounter wrongly labelled or unreliable lithium cells, incomplete product information, incompatible components and insufficient technical guidance. This creates purchasing uncertainty and can lead to poor or unsafe battery and solar builds.

Emax aims to address this through:
- A managed catalogue of products and technical specifications.
- Clear stock-status communication.
- Helpful battery and solar sizing calculators.
- A direct WhatsApp enquiry and sales journey.
- A professionally managed admin system so product information remains current.

## 4. Target users
- DIY lithium battery builders, beginner to advanced
- Solar generator builders
- Solar installers and technicians
- Individuals and companies buying e-bikes
- Wholesale buyers and resellers

## 5. Goals and success measures
- Sell at least 10 e-bikes per month for months 1-6.
- Drive qualified WhatsApp enquiries with contextual prefilled messages.
- Measure funnel milestones: visits -> product views -> calculator use -> WhatsApp clicks -> orders.
