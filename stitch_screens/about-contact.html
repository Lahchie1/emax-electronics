shield Integrity In Nigerian Solar Storage

Eliminating Counterfeits & Relabeled Cells from Nigerian Solar Storage.

In open-air markets across Lagos, Onitsha, and Abuja, an estimated 68% of unregulated lithium battery packs house discarded electric vehicle cells or repackaged Grade-B rejects wrapped in counterfeit heat-shrink sleeves.

These counterfeit modules suffer from violent internal resistance variations, degrading cell lifespans from the promised 6,000 cycles down to fewer than 700 cycles while putting expensive hybrid solar inverters and urban fleet frames at catastrophic failure risk.

Emax Tech operates on a factory-direct depot model. Every single prism and pouch cell received in our Ikeja Engineering Bay is barcoded, logged for resting voltage, and passed through automated binning before being tagged for assembly.
