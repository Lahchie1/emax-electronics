Frequently Asked Questions

How do you guarantee your LiFePO4 cells are genuine Grade-A? +

Every cell features original factory laser-etched QR codes intact. We measure AC internal resistance (<0.25mΩ) using calibrated YR1035+ meters and test open circuit voltage consistency. We welcome customers with their own meters to inspect before payment.

Can I recharge the Emax VeloFlow e-bike with my home solar setup? +

Yes! The included smart charger draws only 120W–150W of power, which works easily with even a small 500VA inverter or small portable solar generator. Full charge takes approximately 4 to 5 hours.

Do you ship to other states outside Lagos? +

Yes, we package all cells and bikes inside heavy foam shock-resistant crating and ship via verified interstate logistics lines (GIGL, Peace Mass Logistics, etc.) with real-time tracking to all 36 states.
