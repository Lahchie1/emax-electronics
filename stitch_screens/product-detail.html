Technician Field Guide

LiFePO4 Safe Assembly Directives

Adhering to these industrial assembly guidelines is required for full validation of Emax batch warranties and ensures safety across high-heat Nigerian ambient conditions.

warning Clamping is Non-Negotiable

Prismatic cells without uniform compression will experience localized delamination and premature capacity loss after 1,000–1,500 cycles.

memory

Active Balancing BMS Required

Use a dedicated Smart BMS with active balancer (JK 16S 200A 2A Active Balance or Daly 250A). Passive balancing resistors are insufficient to maintain balance across large 280Ah banks.

layers

Inter-Cell FR4 Epoxy Separators

Insert 0.5mm FR4 fiberglass sheets between every cell in your series string. Never assemble bare aluminum cell bodies directly side-by-side to prevent vibration shorting.

electric_meter

Class-T or Mega In-Line Fuse

A catastrophic short on a 48V 14.3kWh bank can release over 10,000 amps. Install a Class-T (20kA interrupt) or certified 250A Mega Fuse immediately at the main positive output stud.

sync_alt

Bench Top-Balancing

Wire cells in parallel and charge to 3.65V at 0.05C cutoff before final series assembly, or run active balancing for 72 hours under shallow float conditions before commissioning.
